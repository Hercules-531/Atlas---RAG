# Visual contract

User-directed redesign: OLED black shell and side panels; dark liquid-glass search and controls; locally shipped Starry Night remains the background. The thin compact search bar grows on focus using a composited transform. Scrolling up zooms the painting gently; downward intent reveals the answer. Motion pauses when hidden and respects reduced motion.

The expanded answer occupies most of the viewport on a locally generated warm paper texture. Large serif answer text and a divided source column establish hierarchy. On narrow screens, sources follow a clear horizontal rule. Source numbers jump to focused, opened local evidence with an original-file/PDF-page link. Only sources actually cited in the answer appear in the source column; full disclosure remains in Privacy.

Colors, materials, typography and motion tokens live in frontend/src/theme/tokens.css; layouts are in style.css. The painting is an isolated replaceable component. Answer/citation rendering is isolated from API/state management. No unmeasured performance or privacy guarantee.
