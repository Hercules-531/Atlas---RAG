# Visual contract
User-pinned composition: a quiet reading room around a locally shipped Starry Night. The painting owns the viewport; warm ivory typography and navy controls maintain contrast. The central question is the only primary task. Receipts and answers live in the bottom drawer. Supporting management remains in side panels.

Implement directly from the approved brief. Token source: frontend/src/theme/tokens.css. Background interface: an isolated replaceable component. Motion is restrained transform-only painting drift and a bottom-drawer ease-out. Pause painting motion when hidden; reduced motion removes it. No measured 60fps claim.
